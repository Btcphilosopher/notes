
**TO WHOMSOEVER IT MAY CONCERN**

**Concerning the Assumption of Office at Aureom.AI and the Provision of Remuneration**

Be it known to those whom these matters may concern that I intend to take up the office of Founder and Chief Executive of Aureom.AI, an enterprise of my own founding, conceived through no small measure of labour, thought and perseverance, and established with a view towards the advancement of industry, invention and the betterment of civilisation.

Much has already been undertaken in the founding of this company, and many hours have been given to its works for which no recompense has yet been received. For though an enterprise may have its beginnings in the imagination of one person, its passage into the wider world requires provision of a more material kind.

Whereupon, in assuming the duties and responsibilities of this office, I expect that suitable arrangements shall be made for my remuneration, whether by way of a signing-on bonus, an immediate cash advance, a regular salary, or some proper combination of the three, subject to the necessary financial arrangements and approvals.

Such provision is sought not merely as a reward for labours already undertaken, but as a means whereby I may devote myself wholly to the considerable works that lie ahead, without the hindrance of uncertainty in my personal affairs.

I therefore request that those entrusted with the provision of capital, the administration of accounts, or the financing of new enterprises give this matter their favourable and timely consideration.

For it is a poor custom that the founder of an undertaking should be left without the means of his own maintenance whilst the fruits of his invention are being brought into the world.

Let the terms be fairly reckoned, the obligations plainly set down, and the necessary funds made available upon their agreement.

In good faith, and with confidence in the works yet to come,

**Founder and Chief Executive**  
**Aureom.AI**
