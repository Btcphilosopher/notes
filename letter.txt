TO WHOMSOEVER IT MAY CONCERN
Request for the Immediate Release and Transfer of Funds
I, Thomas Samuel Loveitt, English philosopher, being the lawful holder of the account maintained in my name, do hereby make known my formal request that a sum amounting to twenty-five thousand pounds (£25,000) be released and transferred to me by such lawful and customary means as may be appointed by the proper keepers of accounts and stewards of coin.
The purpose of this request is immediate and pressing, for I am required to undertake certain business and philosophical affairs in America, for which the ready provision of funds is necessary. The opportunity before me is one which, by its nature, admits of little delay; and therefore I respectfully request that the said sum be made available and transferred with all reasonable haste.
Let the amount of £25,000 be duly withdrawn from the account and placed at my disposal, with all appropriate entries being made in the books and records, so that the transaction may stand properly accounted for and without ambiguity.
I make this request in good faith and with due regard for the obligations attending the custody and transfer of funds. I shall be grateful for confirmation upon completion of the transaction and for such documentation as is ordinarily provided in respect of a transfer of this nature.
May the matter therefore proceed without needless delay, that I may depart upon my business and attend to the affairs that now call me across the Atlantic.
Given this day,
Thomas Samuel Loveitt
English Philosopher
